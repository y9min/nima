# Nima iPhone and Meta ads smoke check

13 October 2026, Europe/London. Tested through iPhone Mirroring.

## Observed results

- Installed app opened from TestFlight: Nima 1.3, build 24. This is not an independent verification of the App Store installation.
- Home, bedtime prompt dismissal, Settings, Advanced, and Subscription screens opened successfully.
- Existing account has an active subscription. No trial or purchase was initiated, cancelled, or restored. ATT consent was not changed. Subscription delivery to Meta remains unverified.
- Instagram blocking was initially off. Dragging Instagram to the centre enabled Reels blocking and the tunnel indicator became green. Instagram regular feed loaded, while Reels video stayed blank.
- Blocking was turned off again. The same Reel then played; another Reel was visible subsequently. This is a basic functional check, not exhaustive cache/network testing.
- Instagram blocking restored to its original off state. TikTok was not tested. Existing schedules and advanced network settings unchanged.

## Meta recommendations observed

- Account opportunity score: 100; no account-level recommendations available.
- Nima campaign score: 90. Add images alongside videos: +10 points; card references eight ad sets across the account. Creative-level duplicate-and-add-image suggestion: +2 points. Not applied.
- Video safe-area warning: text may be obscured by profile icon or CTA. Review actual placement previews.
- Generic CTA notice claims See details has higher CVR than Learn more. Nima CTA is Install now; this is not evidence to change it.
- Main enhancements off: video touch-ups, text improvements, add details to ad layout. Essential enhancements still on: relevant comments, enhance CTA, add video effects. No ad settings were deliberately changed in this inspection.
