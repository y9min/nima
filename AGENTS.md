# Paid acquisition testing

- For low-budget app-ad creative tests, give each creative a fair, controlled exposure instead of letting many creatives compete at once.
- Test creatives sequentially in the dedicated broad creative-test campaign: normally keep only one test creative active at a time.
- Aim for roughly 2,000-3,000 impressions per creative, and never judge below 1,000 impressions.
- Use CTR, link CTR, and CPM to reject weak creatives early. Use trials or purchases—not installs alone—to identify winners.
- A promising result after a very small spend is not enough; let the creative complete its exposure batch.
- Keep winners in a separate scaling campaign and raise budgets gradually, around 15-20% at a time.
- Keep the test campaign running for new batches, and improve the store page, onboarding, and paywall when post-click conversion is the constraint.
